| 7 | Caliph Omar ordered the library burned in 641 CE ("redundant with or contrary to the Quran"). | **UNCERTAIN — treat as legend, not history** | Traces entirely to Bar Hebraeus, writing ~600 years after the event, via a secondhand account. ## Disputed / uncertain claims — detail

- **Claim 2a** (Demetrius's role): safe to mention as the traditional account, but hedge
  ("tradition credits Demetrius of Phalerum...") rather than stating flatly.
- **Claim 6b** (Serapeum = library destruction): this is the single most important
  correction from the independent pass. The temple's destruction is real and
  well-documented; its connection to "the library" is not supported by any ancient
  source. The script should present them as two separate facts, not one event.
- **Claim 7** (Caliph Omar): present explicitly as a widely-repeated but
  historically unsupported legend — good material for the script's "what people get
  wrong" angle, not something to state as settled history.
